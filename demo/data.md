# Demo data

[Palmer Penguins](palmer_penguins.csv) was packaged by Allison Horst, Alison Hill and Kristen Gorman. The observations were collected by Kristen Gorman and Palmer Station LTER. The dataset is available under CC0. [Publisher, citation and licence](https://allisonhorst.github.io/palmerpenguins/)

The interactive experiment uses four morphology fields to predict species. It excludes two rows missing one or more of those fields, giving 342 observations. Year provides ordering at annual resolution. A fixed random seed determines order inside each year. The original `penguins.csv` remains the small fixture used by the notebook.

The drift stream is generated by the demo. It flips a synthetic label rule at row 160 and helps illustrate the limitations of an estimator that retains older observations.
